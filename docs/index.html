How to play

Each day has 10 manga, manhwa, or manhua. Guess the title of each one.

  • Every title starts at 1,000 points. You can see its origin, format, popularity, score, genres, and release years for free.
  • Reveal hints to narrow it down. Each hint costs points: cover 50, summary 100, tags 75, author and artist 50, characters 75, each review 50.
  • You get 3 guesses per title. Wrong guesses don't cost points; a correct guess keeps whatever your hints left you. Miss all 3 and the title scores 0, but if your guess was basically right, you can claim "close enough" for 250.
  • Any English or romaji title works. Small typos and words like "the", "a", "of", and "in" don't matter.
  • For sequels, the original series' name also counts.

A new puzzle comes out every day at midnight Central time.
